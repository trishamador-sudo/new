How much does a quote cost?

Nothing. Tell me what your computer is doing, or what you want to spend and what you need it for, and I'll price the parts and the labour. You get the price in writing, and nothing happens until you say go ahead.

Will you tell me if my computer isn't worth fixing?

Yes. If it's not worth fixing, or buying new works out cheaper, I'll tell you that rather than take the repair job.

Do I have to bring my computer in?

You can drop it off, or I can fetch it if you're nearby.

Do you repair laptops and desktop PCs?

Yes. I repair computers that won't switch on or keep restarting, blue screens and Windows problems, overheating and loud fans, cracked screens, keyboards and charging ports.

Can you get my files and photos off a broken computer?

Yes. Data recovery, including getting files off a dying drive, is one of the repairs I do.

What upgrade will make my computer faster?

An SSD is the biggest speed jump you can buy. More RAM helps if it chokes with tabs open, and a new graphics card helps with games and rendering.

Can you build me a gaming PC?

Yes. I build gaming PCs to your budget, as well as office, home, editing and design machines. If you're building it yourself, I can just advise on parts.

Which areas do you cover?

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday from 9am to 7pm, and Saturday from 10am to 4pm. You can also send a WhatsApp or fill in the quote form at any time, and I'll get back to you.
