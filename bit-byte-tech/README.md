# Bit Byte Tech: website mock-up

A two-page mock-up for Bit Byte Tech (computer repairs, upgrades and new builds). It is a static site (HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript). The content comes from the client's three posters in `assets/references/`: `poster.pdf`, `diagnosis-sheet.jpg` and `services-poster.jpg`.

## Pages
- **`index.html`:** the main page. Its sections are:
  - hero;
  - professional computer services, with the CompTIA A+ badge;
  - services: repairs and diagnostics, upgrades and new computers;
  - the Diagnosis Sheet: symptoms, treatment plan, "Most cases are not terminal", and "The quote costs you nothing";
  - "Need a new computer?";
  - why choose Bit Byte Tech;
  - contact, with call, WhatsApp, email and the QR code;
  - the "Reliable tech" strip.
- **`contact.html`:** the contact form (page 2), with the contact details and the QR code.

## Editing
The pages are generated by `build.py`, which holds the shared header, footer and content. Edit it, then run `python3 build.py`. Styles are in `styles.css`.

## Design
- **Colours (from the logo):** near-black navy `#070C13`, electric blue `#39A9F5` / `#37B8FF`, and white. The whole site is dark.
- **Fonts:** all self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`.
  - Exo 2 for headings.
  - Manrope for body text.
  - Yellowtail for the "Friendly Neighbourhood Geek" line.
  - Courier Prime for the Diagnosis Sheet.

## Mock-up only
- **Contact form:** not connected. It checks the fields and shows a thank-you message, but sends nothing.
- **Contact details:** the phone, WhatsApp and email appear as text with icons, not as links. Buttons go to the contact page.
- **Domain:** assumed to be `https://www.bitbytetech.co.za`. Update `DOMAIN` in `build.py`, `robots.txt` and the sitemap once the real domain is known.
- **Not on the posters, left out:** the owner's name, address, service area and hours.
