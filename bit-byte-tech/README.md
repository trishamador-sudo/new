# Bit Byte Tech: website mock-up

A two-page mock-up for Bit Byte Tech (computer repairs, upgrades and new builds). It is a static site (HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript). The content comes from the client's three posters in `assets/references/`: `poster.pdf`, `diagnosis-sheet.jpg` and `services-poster.jpg`.

## Page
One page, `index.html`, with the quote form at the top on the right so the call to action is the first thing people see. The page is laid out as follows:
- **Hero:** on the left, the logo, the headline, the "Call 076 803 0406" and "WhatsApp me" buttons and the badges. On the right, the **Get a free quote** form.
- **Sections:** professional services with the CompTIA A+ badge, services, the Diagnosis Sheet, "Need a new computer?", why choose Bit Byte Tech, contact (call, WhatsApp, email and QR) and the "Reliable tech" strip.
- **Free-quote buttons:** every "Get a free quote" button jumps to the form.
- **Phones:** a sticky Call / WhatsApp / Free quote bar sits at the bottom of the screen.
- **Contact details:** the phone number, WhatsApp and email are clickable (tap to call, open WhatsApp, or send an email).

## Editing
The pages are generated by `build.py`, which holds the shared header, footer and content. Edit it, then run `python3 build.py`. Styles are in `styles.css`.

## Design
- **Colours (from the logo):** near-black navy `#070C13`, electric blue `#39A9F5` / `#37B8FF`, and white. The whole site is dark.
- **Fonts:** all self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`.
  - Exo 2 for headings.
  - Manrope for body text.
  - Yellowtail for the "Friendly Neighbourhood Geek" line.
  - Courier Prime for the Diagnosis Sheet.

## Contact form (Formspree)
The quote form sends to `https://formspree.io/f/xaeerwne` (set in `FORM_ENDPOINT` at the top of `main.js`). Messages arrive at the email address that owns the Formspree form. The first submission sends a confirmation email to that address; click the link in it to activate the form. A hidden spam-trap field blocks simple bots.

## Mock-up notes
- **Domain:** assumed to be `https://www.bitbytetech.co.za`. Update `DOMAIN` in `build.py`, `robots.txt` and the sitemap once the real domain is known.
- **Not on the posters, left out:** the owner's name, address, service area and hours.
