Service

Home renovations by an English-speaking builder in the Alentejo

Buying an older house in the Alentejo is exciting, until you need a builder who answers the phone, speaks your language and tells you the truth about timings. We are a dad-and-daughter renovation team based in Montemor-o-Novo, and we speak English.

Get a free quote Chat on WhatsApp

We renovate houses, quintas, montes and apartments, from refreshing a single room to a full renovation. Traditional Alentejo houses have thick walls, uneven floors and decades of repairs layered on top of each other. We plan around what we find, explain it clearly and put the price in writing before we start.

Because we are a small family team, the person who quotes your job is the person who looks after it. You always know who you are talking to and what happens next.

What's included

  • Full house, quinta and apartment renovations
  • New flooring, including wood-effect flooring
  • Feature walls, slatted panels and wall finishes
  • Bathroom and kitchen renovations
  • Interior and exterior painting
  • Bespoke carpentry, such as wardrobes and cupboards
  • Renovations for shops, offices and holiday lets

A good example is the hallway we renovated: we lifted the old tiles, laid wood-effect flooring, built a slatted feature wall with a floating shelf and repainted throughout. It feels modern now, without losing the character of the house.

Questions people ask

Questions about home renovations

Can't see your question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do you take on small jobs or only big renovations?

Both. We can refresh a hallway or a single room, or renovate a whole house. Tell us what you need and we will tell you honestly if and when we can help.

Can you work while we are abroad?

Yes. Many of our English-speaking clients are not in Portugal full time. We send updates and photos on WhatsApp so you can follow the work from wherever you are.

Do you do electrical work?

No, we do not do electrical work. If your renovation needs an electrician, we will tell you at the start.
