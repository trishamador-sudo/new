## Blog posts / news

### 1. Reminder: PAIA submission deadline approaching
Under Section 83(4) of PAIA, private bodies are requested to submit their PAIA
reports to the Information Regulator.
- The deadline for PAIA submissions is the 30th of June 2026.
- Submissions must be made via the Information Regulator's e-Services portal.
- Need help? Contact Lawgical Labour for expert assistance with the process.
NOTE: this deadline has passed. Update or reframe as an annual reminder.

### 2. Annual leave forfeiture
"Any leave balance not taken within 18 months of the leave-earning period is
legally forfeited, unless you can prove your employer actively blocked you
from taking it."

### 3. Important announcement: National Minimum Wage
On 3 February 2026, the Minister of Employment and Labour announced the
increase of South Africa's National Minimum Wage to R30,23 per hour,
effective 1 March 2026.
Contact us for expert labour law advice.

### 4. The Draft Dismissal Code: what you need to know
No major changes, mere rewording of existing laws.
- Fair procedure still required
- Small businesses still need to follow due process
- A decrease in CCMA cases not foreseen
For expert advice and guidance in handling all labour related matters.
