Blog · Labour law updates

What employers need to know

Wages · Effective 1 March 2026

National Minimum Wage increases to R30,23 an hour

On 3 February 2026, the Minister of Employment and Labour announced an increase in South Africa's National Minimum Wage to R30,23 per hour, effective 1 March 2026. Check your payroll and contracts against the new rate.

Leave

Annual leave: use it or lose it after 18 months

Any leave balance not taken within 18 months of the leave-earning period is legally forfeited, unless the employee can prove the employer actively blocked them from taking it. Keep clear leave records.

Dismissals

The Draft Dismissal Code: what you need to know

No major changes, mostly a rewording of existing law. Fair procedure is still required, small businesses still need to follow due process, and a drop in CCMA cases isn't expected.

Compliance · Annual reminder

PAIA: your yearly report to the Information Regulator

Under section 83(4) of PAIA, private bodies are requested to submit their PAIA reports to the Information Regulator each year via the e-Services portal. Diarise the deadline and confirm the current date with the Regulator.
