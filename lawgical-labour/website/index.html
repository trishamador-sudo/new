Blog · Labour law updates

What employers need to know

Wages · Effective 1 March 2026

National Minimum Wage increases to R30,23 an hour

On 3 February 2026, the Minister of Employment and Labour announced an increase in South Africa's National Minimum Wage to R30,23 per hour, effective 1 March 2026.

What to do

Check your payroll and employment contracts against the new rate. Contact us for expert labour law advice.

Leave

Annual leave: use it or lose it after 18 months

Any leave balance not taken within 18 months of the leave-earning period is legally forfeited, unless the employee can prove the employer actively blocked them from taking it.

What to do

Keep clear leave records and make sure employees have a fair opportunity to take their leave. Need help with your leave policy? Talk to us.

Dismissals

The Draft Dismissal Code: what you need to know

No major changes, mostly a rewording of existing law. Fair procedure is still required, small businesses still need to follow due process, and a drop in CCMA cases isn't expected.

What to do

Keep following fair procedure for every dismissal. For expert advice and guidance in handling all labour-related matters, contact us.

Compliance · Annual reminder

PAIA: your yearly report to the Information Regulator

Under section 83(4) of PAIA, private bodies are requested to submit their PAIA reports to the Information Regulator each year, via the Regulator's e-Services portal.

What to do

Diarise the annual deadline and confirm the current date with the Information Regulator. Need help? Contact Lawgical Labour for expert assistance with the process.
