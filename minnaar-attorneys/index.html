About the firm

A personal approach to labour law.

Labour matters affect livelihoods, workplaces and reputations. They deserve careful handling and advice you can understand.

At Minnaar Attorneys you work directly with Louise, from the first call to the final outcome. We explain where you stand, what your options are and what each step involves, then handle the process properly.

  • Personal interaction

    You deal directly with your attorney, not a call centre.

  • Plain language

    Labour law explained clearly, so you can make informed decisions.

  • Done properly

    Notices, procedures and documents prepared with attention to detail.

  • Fair fees

    Professional service at fair prices, with a fee estimate up front.
