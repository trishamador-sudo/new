Working with us

Do you only see clients in Krugersdorp?

No. Our office is in Krugersdorp, Gauteng, where we see clients by appointment. We also consult online, so we can assist clients all over South Africa.

How do I book a consultation?

Call or WhatsApp 084 927 0101, email louise@minnaar-law.co.za, or send the consultation form on this page. Tell us briefly what your matter is about and we'll arrange a time that suits you, in person or online.

What legal matters do you assist with?

Commercial law, employment and labour law, contract law, general legal matters and legal documentation. If you're not sure whether we can help with your matter, ask us.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Thursday 08:00–16:00 and Friday 08:00–15:00. We are closed on weekends and public holidays.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Any documents related to your matter, such as contracts, letters, emails, notices or court papers, and a short timeline of what happened. If we're meeting online, you can send these to us beforehand.

Protection of Personal Information (POPIA)

Minnaar Attorneys respects your privacy and processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Information you give us through this website, the consultation form, email, WhatsApp or by phone (such as your name, contact details and a description of your matter) is used only to respond to your enquiry and, if you instruct us, to provide legal services to you.

We keep your information confidential and secure, and we do not sell it. We share it only where needed to provide our services (for example, our website form is processed by our form provider, Formspree) or where the law requires it. You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its processing, by emailing louise@minnaar-law.co.za. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

The information on this website is general and is not legal advice. Please contact us for advice on your specific circumstances.

Emails from Minnaar Attorneys and any attachments are confidential and intended solely for the addressee. If you received one in error, please notify the sender and delete it.
