## 1. Name and slogan
- **Name:** Minnaar Attorneys. The logo reads "MINNAAR" with a black "ATTORNEYS" bar.
- **Slogan:** *Moving beyond conflict.*

## 2. Positioning and audience
- **What:** a law firm offering professional legal services, not a product.
- **Where:** based in **Krugersdorp, West Rand, Gauteng**. Consults online and serves clients across **all of South Africa**.
- **Who:** businesses and individuals who need commercial, contract, employment/labour and general legal advice, and legal documentation.
- **Director:** Louise Minnaar (Botha), Director | Attorney, LLB (North-West University).

## 3. Personality
**Premium and fierce.** Calm authority; decisive and protective of the client; never flashy.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Premium, composed, precise | Loud, salesy, cluttered |
| Fierce in protecting the client | Aggressive or hostile |
| Plain-spoken | Full of legalese |

## 5. Typography
Same as Lawgical Labour: **Manrope** (self-hosted) for headings (700–800) and body (400–600). The **Cinzel** caps are kept only for the "MINNAAR / ATTORNEYS" wordmark, to match the logo.

## 6. Logo
- Burgundy serif M with scales, an Ionic column and a quill, "MINNAAR" and a black "ATTORNEYS" bar.
- On dark backgrounds, use the white mark (`assets/img/mark-white.png`).
- The hero image already carries the logo watermark at top left, so keep text clear of that area.

## 7. Offering (legal services)
1. Commercial law
2. Labour and employment law
3. Contract law
4. General legal matters
5. Legal documentation

The services are offered in person in Krugersdorp and online across South Africa.

## 8. Key messages and SEO
**Primary words to use on the first screen:** commercial lawyer, contract law, employment law, labour law, attorney, Krugersdorp, West Rand, South Africa.

**Proof points:** admitted attorney; personal service from the director; online consultations nationwide.

## 9. Visual mood
Sophisticated and simple. A **dark, moody atmosphere**: black and charcoal, dramatic light, marble columns, bronze scales, and the crimson quill as the single colour accent. No background video.

## 10. Landing page goal
**High converting, with as many contact options as possible:**
- call;
- WhatsApp;
- email;
- the consultation form (Formspree `https://formspree.io/f/xoevogrd`);
- online consultation;
- Krugersdorp by appointment.

A mobile action bar and a floating WhatsApp button keep these one tap away.
