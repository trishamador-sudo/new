# House of Ahern: website concept

A one-page sample design for House of Ahern, Rhodesian Ridgeback breeders. It is a static page (HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript) to show the breeder what their website could look like.

## Design
- **Look:** dark editorial layout, after the inspiration image (`assets/references/inspiration.png`).
- **Colours:** black background, a green info bar and green script headings (crest green `#1F5709` and lighter shades), with rust `#AC592F` buttons, taupe and cream from the brand palette.
- **Fonts:**
  - Pinyon Script for the large script words, to match the crest's "House of Ahern" lettering;
  - Cormorant Garamond for subheadings;
  - Montserrat for body text.

  All three are self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`.
- **Images:** the crest and the dog photos are cut out on transparent backgrounds in `assets/img/`. The originals are in `assets/references/`.

## Sections
1. Hero: "Ridgebacks", House of Ahern, Rhodesian Ridgeback breeders.
2. 01–04: What to expect, Bred with purpose, Raised at home, Family for life.
3. 05 The breed: "Courageous", general information about the Rhodesian Ridgeback.
4. 06 Our puppies: a photo carousel, plus Puppy pack, Home sweet home and Vet check.
5. The family: a photo gallery.
6. Join the family: the crest and a puppy enquiry form.

## Before this goes live
- **Sample copy:** the text is sample copy. Confirm every claim with the breeder (for example the puppy pack contents, vet checks and support) and add real details: location, contact details, registrations, health testing, and upcoming litters.
- **Enquiry form:** it only shows a thank-you message. Connect it to Formspree (or similar).
- **Live-site files:** add a domain, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, a canonical link and absolute Open Graph URLs once the site is going live.
