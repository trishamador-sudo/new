CourageousThe Rhodesian Ridgeback

The Rhodesian Ridgeback was developed in southern Africa as a versatile hunting and guardian dog. It is named for the ridge of hair along its back, which grows in the opposite direction to the rest of its coat.

Ridgebacks are strong and athletic, with a short wheaten coat. They are loyal and affectionate with their family, often reserved with strangers, and thrive on companionship, exercise and gentle, consistent training.
